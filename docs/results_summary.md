## Interpretation

The fair index controls for channel, format and age group. It reduces—but cannot eliminate—confounding from topic, season, audience, recommendation systems, paid distribution and production strategy.

The findings are correlations. They are not evidence that changing upload time, duration or title wording will cause more views.
